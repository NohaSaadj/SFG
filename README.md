## How the SFG intro works (v8)
The intro **plays on its own**. The four photos get the same time on screen and every photo change is a quick 0.7s crossfade (`AUTO.photoChangeSeconds`; before, the third change was quicker than the others because it overlapped the sentence moving left). Each part runs at its own pace: the paragraph rolls at about one line every 1.3s so it can be read (about 30s on desktop, longer on phones where it has more lines), the sentence moves left in 1.0s, holds for 0.8s, then the fade to the closing line takes 1.6s. Scrolling down (mouse wheel, trackpad, touch or arrow keys) speeds it up; through the paragraph, scrolling is damped a little so it stays readable. Scrolling up plays it back, and after 1.4 seconds without input it carries on forward again. The pace values sit together in `AUTO` at the top of the script.

1. **Opening (2s):** a dark screen with only the first sentence, in the middle of the page. Then the rest of the paragraph, the photos, the navbar and "Skip Intro" fade in.
2. **Roll:** the paragraph rises and hides from the middle of the screen up. Each hidden-sentence word lifts out where the paragraph starts to hide and builds the sentence above, at full paragraph size and on two lines.
3. **Photos:** each one gets an equal share of the time until the sentence is complete. Each one slowly zooms in, then crossfades into the next. The order is Imperial, UCD (centred), Aberdeen, LSE. The UCD frame (`assets/student-2.webp`) is centred, with soft side streaks stretched from the photo's own edges, like the Aberdeen and LSE frames; the strip carrying the SFG watermark was cropped off the top, since the header already carries the logo.
4. **Sentence and zoom-out together:** once the roll is done, the sentence moves in one go to its left-aligned place, still on two lines. At the same time the LSE photo zooms out. On phones the sentence is set at 24px so it stays on two lines.
5. **Hold:** the sentence holds briefly on the settled photo.
6. **Ending:** the photo fades to 15% and the sentence to 0%. Halfway through, "Here’s how the story begins." rises from the bottom into the place the sentence left.
7. **Next section:** the page unlocks. Scrolling back to the top re-enters the intro and plays it backwards.

**Arabic:** the text sits on the right, so Arabic shows the same LSE photo mirrored, with the student on the left. This keeps «فلسطين متعلّمة» and «هكذا تبدأ الحكاية» clear of her face. Because it is mirrored, the LSE signs read backwards in the Arabic version.

The photos are wide 1920×1080 frames. Each is shown at its real resolution, and the sides are filled from the photo's own edge colours with a light blur. There are no ghosted copies and nothing is pixelated. `index.html` has them embedded, so it works as a single file. `index.src.html` is the same page with links to `assets/` instead, for editing.

## Partners (after the intro, before About)
Laid out like Cambium's "Associated sustainability organizations" band, made as compact as possible: one centred row of four 192×64px tiles: the label "Our partners", the two logos, and "Become a partner". 4px gaps show the background. The band is 128px tall on desktop (32px above and below) and 180px on phones (a 2×2 grid). The background is the traced tatreez band tiled in offset rows over the whole band (a vector `<pattern>`), in two quiet neutrals (`--color-pattern-stitch` = border, `--color-pattern-stitch-soft` = sand), so it reads as texture like Cambium's wood grain and the logos keep their contrast.
- Logos: Phoenix Space and the Amjad & Suha Bseisu Foundation (`assets/partners/`). Both are single-colour masks filled with `--color-text-primary`, so they are ink in light mode and linen in dark mode.
- The third tile, "Become a partner", is a green text link (`#partner-with-us`, placeholder until there is a partner page or address). Add more partners as new tiles.
The caption and link are translated for Arabic.

## Reveals (after cambium.com)
Cambium's own animation scripts can't be reached from this environment, so the motion was rebuilt from the `data-module="split-text"` / `data-reveal-*` markup on their home page:
- **Text (`data-split`), page-wide:** the About reveal now runs on every section heading and text block: About, How SFG began, What we do (the three columns, in place of their fade-up), How it works, the Results title and cards, the Results text, the Student voices title (in place of its own mask slide) and In Remembrance (title and each letter paragraph). The intro, the "What we don't do" sheet (its own pen-mark reveal), card quotes and captions keep their own motion. Inner markup (names kept whole, links) is restored when a reveal ends. Headings and paragraphs are split into lines. Each line rises out of a mask (1.1s, ease-out-expo), 0.08s after the line before it. Inside a `data-split-group` the stagger carries on from heading to text. It plays once, when the block enters the screen; afterwards the text goes back to plain text so it reflows normally.
- **Section 2 steps:** the year, title and text rise line by line each time a step becomes active, on desktop and in the mobile slider.
- **Photos (`data-reveal-media`):** each frame uncovers from the bottom while the photo settles from 118% to 100%.
- **Details:** the tatreez band wipes in from the start side (`data-reveal-wipe`); the pager fades up (`data-reveal`).
- With reduced motion turned on, everything is shown at once. Without JavaScript nothing is hidden.

## How it works (cloned from cambium.com/technology, section 2)
A clone of Cambium's "process line" section (`how_wrap`: Sourcing → Transport → Processing → Buy and build → An industry that grows back), with the same structure and class names, applied to SFG's five steps and mapped to the design system. The reference notes, measurements and motion spec are in `reference/cambium-technology/notes.md`. A standalone clone with Cambium's own copy, for comparison, is in `clone/cambium-technology-how/index.html`.
- **Divider:** the section opens with the tatreez band from "How SFG began", running from margin to margin (not full width). The script fits a whole number of motifs to the width and scales them evenly, so the band never ends on a cut motif. It wipes in like the hem.
- **Structure (as Cambium):** each `how_layout` row has the text (`how_content`: the dot `how_content_anchor`, then `how_text_wrap` with heading, paragraph and, on the last step, a button) on one side and the picture (`how_visual`) on the other, alternating sides. Text is centred and vertically centred on the picture. Text in columns 1–5 (or 8–12), picture in columns 6–12 (or 1–7), held on the page margin; rows 192px apart (`--space-192`, 128px on phones).
- **The line:** one thin line (`--color-line-process`, ink at 24%) through every step's dot, vertical at each dot with an S-curve between the two sides. It is drawn with the scroll: it starts when the first dot reaches mid-screen and is complete when the last dot does, or when the page can scroll no further, so it always reaches the last step. Behind each text block (`how_text_bg`) the line fades out under the dot and back in below the text; this is done with a soft mask on the line, so the background is never painted over (no visible box in dark mode).
- **Pictures:** fixed on the page margin while scrolling (no drift). Each picture slot keeps its own ratio (`--r`), up to 7 columns wide and 560px tall, so any photo fits uncropped. Behind each picture sits the same field of neutral tatreez stitches as the partners band (`#stitchField`), on linen with a thin border. Until SFG has photos, each slot shows an illustration of the step, drawn at a 520px design height so the card covers about 70–78% of its frame and the embroidery shows around it (application form, mentor match, shortlist, draft with red-pen notes, submitted checklist).
- **SFG mapping:** linen background (Cambium's #FAF2E7 is almost identical), type hierarchy by weight: the "How it works" eyebrow in green Title3 (Sans Medium 16px), the section heading H2 Bold (48px, ink), step headings H2 size in Medium 500 (ink), descriptions Body2 (16px Regular, muted, 40ch). Headings use neutral ink (`--color-text-primary`), not green; dots in the mentor's red pen (12px, solid), and one secondary green button on the last step ("Start your application"). The section keeps SFG's opening (eyebrow, H2, text), which Cambium's section doesn't have because its hero sits right above it.
- **Fonts:** the medium step headings need Thmanyah Serif Display Medium (500) bundled next to Bold; the Newsreader fallback now loads 500 too.
- **Phones:** one column, centred text with the picture underneath, and the line running straight down the middle behind the text and pictures. Illustrations are shown at full size. Reduced motion: line drawn in full, no drift.
- **Students' next step (no separate For students section), in step 1 "Apply to SFG":** a green **Start your application** button (mentee application form `https://secure.lglforms.com/form_engine/s/gj0TRCbaEZdlSZIsRp5jKg`, new tab) and under it a quieter text link **"Not ready yet? Read our student guide (in Arabic)"** (`https://docs.google.com/document/d/13uX0ClWeqEinlqcHFDqgCYSa4T1SQ60xR9QBD7iY3Jw/edit`, `hreflang="ar"`, new tab). The guide is an Arabic FAQ and toolkit (documents per degree, CV and letters, English tests, university contacts, scholarship tips), so it has one entry point where students decide; it should also go in the footer. Green, because red stays with Become a mentor. The header's "For students" jumps to How it works.

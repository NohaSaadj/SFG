About SFG

A Palestinian-led mentorship initiative

Scholarships for Ghazza is a Palestinian-led mentorship initiative. We pair students from Gaza with volunteer mentors around the world, who guide them through every step of applying for a fully funded scholarship, from choosing a programme to the final interview. With Gaza’s universities destroyed, studying abroad is how these students keep learning, and how they will rebuild.

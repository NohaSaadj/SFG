1. Sourcing

    We’ve built a network of landowners and forest data so that we can source locally across the country. Every material on Cambium is sourced from suppliers we've vetted for sustainable forest management and manufacturingpractices.

  2. Transport

    Our network gets stronger with every board. Mill Market is our platform that coordinates arborists, haulers, and mills, matching on-demand jobs with intelligent routing across the wood supply chain.

  3. Processing

    A national network of sawmills and partners turns the wood into next-gen material. We bring steady demand to these local businesses, creating custom supply chains to drive business their way.

  4. Buy and build

    We distribute wood for virtually any use, at any scale. Our end-to-end network allows our team and our partners to develop and move innovative materials that traditional supply chains can’t carry, like our own flagship product, Carbon Smart Wood.

  5. An industry that grows back

    Wood should be our most sustainable resource. Every sale on Cambium funds replanting and drives down the carbon impact of the wood industry by delivering local sourcing at national scale. With every material we move, we prove that wood can do what steel and concrete can't: renew itself to build a better world for all of us.
